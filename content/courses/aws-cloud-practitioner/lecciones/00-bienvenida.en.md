## 📘 Content

### Welcome

This course prepares you for the **AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02)**
exam, the entry point into AWS certifications. You don't need to know how to
code, or have used the cloud before — this course is built for exactly that
starting point.

### What is "the cloud", in plain words?

Before the electrical grid existed, every factory needed its own generator to
have electricity. Building and maintaining that generator was expensive, took
up space, and if the factory grew, it had to buy a bigger generator.

Today you just plug a cable into the wall and pay for the electricity you use,
without worrying about how it's generated.

Cloud computing works the same way, but with technology: instead of buying and
maintaining your own servers, you "connect" to a provider's infrastructure —
in this case, AWS — and pay only for what you use. AWS takes care of building
and maintaining the "generators" (servers, networks, data centers); you just
turn on what you need, when you need it.

That's essentially the whole topic of **Module 1** of this course — here we
just wanted you to walk away with a first, concrete idea before moving on.

### How the course is organized

The course follows exactly the exam's 4 official domains, plus a welcome
module and a final review module:

| Module | Content | Exam weight |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Welcome and setup | — |
| 1 | Cloud Concepts | 24% |
| 2 | Security and Compliance | 30% |
| 3 | Cloud Technology and Services | 34% |
| 4 | Billing, Pricing, and Support | 12% |
| 5 | Final review and practice exam | — |

Each content module (1 through 4) ends with a **comparison tables** lesson —
where we put similar services side by side (for example, "when do I use S3
and when do I use EBS?"), which is the type of question that shows up most
often on the real exam — and an **exam question breakdown** lesson, 20
questions with the real exam format so you can practice spotting the traps
while the module is still fresh.

### How each lesson is structured

Every lesson in the course — including this one — follows the same
structure, always in the same order:

1. **🤔 Before we start** — a couple of questions so your brain starts
   looking for the answer as you read, even if you don't know it yet.
2. **📘 Content** — the explanation, with everyday examples before the
   technical ones.
3. **💬 Now it's your turn** — the same questions from the beginning, so you
   can answer them now that you've read the topic.
4. **🎯 Exam hints** — how to think about that topic for the exam, not new
   content.

### How you'll move forward

At the end of each lesson you'll find two controls:

- **Mark as complete** — to keep track of what you've already seen.
- **Go to next lesson** — takes you to the next one in the course order.

For now, that progress is saved in your browser (localStorage), tied to your
user. Later on it'll be saved in a database, so it doesn't depend on which
browser you're using — but how you use it won't change.

**In short:** this course follows the exam's 4 official CLF-C02 domains, every
lesson has the same 4-part structure, and your progress is saved as you go.

## 🎯 Exam hints

- The exam mixes domains within a single question — you won't find "now
  comes a security-only question." That's why the order of the modules
  matters less than understanding each topic well on its own.
- Don't skip the comparison-table lessons (★) or the question-breakdown
  lessons: they're exactly the format that shows up most in real CLF-C02
  questions.
- Before looking at the "Suggested answer" in each lesson, actually try to
  answer — even out loud or just in your head. That's what will help you
  remember the topic most on exam day.
